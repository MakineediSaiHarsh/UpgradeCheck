The problem

The repair quote is only one part of the decision.

When your fridge needs repairing, you may be wondering whether to fix it or buy the larger or frost-free model you really want. UpgradeCheck will help you compare those choices. We’re starting with fridges, then adding a separate check for ACs.

A preview of the first check

What would an upgrade need to save?

Suppose a fridge repair is quoted at ₹4,000 and the new model you want costs ₹25,000. The new purchase requires ₹21,000 more upfront. Here is the electricity saving it would need to recover that gap over five years.

This is a worked example, not a recommendation. A bigger fridge may offer more space and convenience while using more electricity.

One decision, different appliances

Start focused. Expand with the right maths.

UpgradeCheck is designed for household repair decisions. Each appliance has different label data and usage patterns, so each check needs its own inputs.

Built for an honest answer

Show the trade-offs. Leave the guesses out.

The eventual tool will compare scenarios. Capacity, convenience, warranty and repair uncertainty remain part of a household’s decision.

Labels are a starting point

BEE label values come from standard tests. Actual use varies by appliance, condition and household. See BEE’s labelling programme.

No invented old-appliance reading

If a label is gone, a break-even target can still help. Age or star rating alone cannot establish how much electricity that individual appliance uses now.

Your tariff and future repair scenarios

An editable electricity price range can illustrate different bill assumptions. Any additional repair cost would be something you choose to test, not a predicted failure.
